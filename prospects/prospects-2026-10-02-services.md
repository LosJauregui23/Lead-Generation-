## Gaps in this run

- **Home services are a big part of this list, but their triggers are weak.** HVAC, roofing and landscaping companies don't hold ribbon cuttings. Hiring was the best signal I could find. Their budgets are usually the largest on the list, so don't skip Tier B because of the label.
- **Some leads probably already run ads.** Update Heating, Valley View Dental and My Smiles Dental all invest visibly in marketing. Check the Meta Ad Library and Google Ads Transparency Center first. If they're running ads, open with an audit, not a pitch from scratch.
- **Financial services came up nearly empty.** Most insurance agents are captive State Farm or Allstate agents, who have tight ad rules. Independent agencies and mortgage brokers need their own search.
- **I dropped one lead I couldn't verify.** A "Stack Heating & Cooling" turned up hiring in New Lenox, but every source for that name pointed to a company in Cleveland, Ohio.
