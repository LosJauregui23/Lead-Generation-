# Vape vending placements: bars within ~30 mi of Joliet, IL (2026-10-12)

**Legal gate first.** Federal rule (21 CFR 1140) only allows nicotine vending in places that never let anyone under 21 in. Illinois (720 ILCS 675/1(b)) also allows bars with owner/manager supervision, but the federal rule is the stricter one. So a bar & grill that seats kids is out. Ask every owner: "Do you ever let under-21s in, even with parents or for food?" Also need an IDOR retail tobacco license for each location.
