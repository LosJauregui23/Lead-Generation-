## Gaps in this run

- **I didn't check whether anyone is already running ads.** That's the most useful signal for a paid media pitch, and this environment blocks the Meta Ad Library and Google Ads Transparency Center. Check each Tier A lead there before reaching out. If they run no ads, that's an easy opener. Bad ads give you an audit pitch. Good ads probably mean they already have an agency.
- **Most domains are marked "not verified."** Local news pages were blocked too, so I only listed sites that showed up in results.
- **Employee counts are unknown.** Lots of these are micro businesses, so budgets may be $500 to $1,500 a month. Price accordingly.
- **The "none found" leads in Tier B could still be good.** They're established, review-rich businesses in high-ticket verticals that buy ads all the time. The difference is there's no event to hook an opener on.
