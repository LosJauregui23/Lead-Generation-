## Gaps in this run

- **I still haven't checked whether anyone is already running ads.** Check the Meta Ad Library and Google Ads Transparency Center for each Tier A lead before you call. That's the opener: no ads, bad ads, or "already has an agency, skip."
- **Employee counts are unknown.** Most of these are micro businesses, so think $500 to $1,500 a month in ad spend.
- **Home services and law came up thin on triggers.** Those businesses don't do ribbon cuttings. The way to find a trigger for them is to look for job postings or bad reviews, which is a separate search.
